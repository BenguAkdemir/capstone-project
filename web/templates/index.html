    What does the system do?

    This tool plans who works onsite vs. remote for each day of the week. It uses your constraints and preferences to build a balanced schedule.

    Hard rules (always enforced)

    • Availability: Employees are only assigned onsite on checked days.
    • Office capacity: Daily onsite headcount cannot exceed the limit you set.
    • Min–max days: Each employee has weekly min/max onsite day targets.
    • Collaboration: Optional minimum department headcount per day.

    What the optimizer improves

    Approximate priority order:

    1. Minimum onsite days — Meet each employee’s minimum target.
    2. Idle capacity — Reduce unused office slots.
    3. Preferences — Honor prefer/avoid day choices when possible.

    Preferences are soft constraints; capacity and collaboration rules take precedence.

    Database & background jobs

    Employee records are stored in PostgreSQL (employees, employee_availability, etc.). When you generate a schedule, the form is synced by employee_id. A weekly job (Mondays 06:00) normalizes availability rows for the new week.
